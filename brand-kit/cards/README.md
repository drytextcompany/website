# Visiting cards — The Dry Text Co.

Nine cards, print-ready. Built from the brand kit in `brand-kit/assets`: same paper, ink and red,
same typefaces, the real wordmarks.

## What to send a printer

Send the PDF for the card you want. **Page 1 is the front, page 2 is the back.**

The page *is* the card plus 3 mm of bleed on every edge — no white margin, no crop marks, no
caption. The trim sits 3 mm inside the page edge, so the printer cuts 3 mm in on all four sides.
Fonts are embedded as proper subsets, so upload portals accept these.

`previews/` holds a 300 dpi PNG of every side, trimmed, for approvals and WhatsApp. Not for print.

## Say this to the printer

- **Stock:** 300–350 gsm uncoated, natural white. No gloss lamination: the whole brand is paper.
- **Colours:** Paper `#FBFBF9`, Ink `#16161A`, Red `#D2232A`. Match the red to the hex.
  Pantone 186 C is the nearest standard red; ask for a draw-down before a long run.
- **The four solid cards** (`h8`, `h9`, `h9b`, `v8`) are heavy ink coverage. Ask for a stock that
  takes it without cracking, and expect them slightly darker than the screen.
- **If the press works in 90 × 55 mm** rather than 88.9 × 50.8, say so and the nine get re-set.
  These files are not the right size for that press.
