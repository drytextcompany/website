How an order gets cooked

No mystery kitchen. Here is exactly what happens between your brief and your final draft.

01

You order

Tell us the brand, the goal and the deadline. We come back with a straight answer, usually within a couple of hours.

02

Calibration

If the scope is fuzzy, we run a short calibration phase first to learn your voice. If it is already clear, we skip straight to project pricing.

03

Prep

Strategy before sentences. Angles, structure, hooks and the calendar get built before a single line is written.

04

On the heat

We write, then we cut. You get an update every few days, by text or a quick call, whichever you prefer.

05

Served

Revisions come back the same day or the next. Once it is paid for, the work and every draft behind it belongs to you.

Table manners

House rules

The boring parts, written plainly, so nobody has to guess.

Revisions

No cap on revisions when there is a genuine reason for the change. A full rewrite from scratch is a bigger ask, so that one has a limit.

Turnaround

Normal revisions come back the same day or the next. A rewrite from a blank page takes a few days, and we say so upfront.

Replies

Email or WhatsApp, answered fast. Urgent things go straight to the person handling your account. Festivals and holidays run slower, honestly.

Your data

Nothing lives on our devices without your permission, and only the person on your account can see it. When the work ends, we wipe it and hand every login back.

Credit

We rarely ask for a byline. In our own portfolio we name the client and the service, never the actual content, unless you say otherwise.

Exclusivity

Not asked for by default. If you want it, we keep it narrow: your named competitors, and the person on your account, not the whole kitchen.

Behind the counter

An independent kitchen, not another desk in your office.

We are not part of your team and we are not a full service agency either. We work as an independent kitchen: we figure out what you actually need, then we cook it.

Right now the agency is run by its founder, who personally handles or oversees every client. That means one point of contact, no handovers, and no filler hours on an invoice. As we grow, dedicated cooks and a proper backup system come in for each account.
