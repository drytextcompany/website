Before you call

Questions people ask first.

How much does content writing cost in Ahmedabad?
Per project, not per word. A social post starts at Rs 1,200, a website page at Rs 4,800, a deck at Rs 11,500, and the price depends on how much research and rewriting the piece needs. You get the exact number in writing before we start, and if the job grows we tell you before it grows.
Do you write in Gujarati as well as English?
Yes, and not by running English through a translator. We have written bag lines and a whole fortnightly newspaper concept in Gujarati, and LinkedIn posts and outreach mails in English, in the same month.
Do you work with brands outside Ahmedabad?
Most of our work is for brands somewhere else. Ahmedabad is where we sit, not who we write for. Briefs come over a call, work comes back the same way.
Can you build the website too, not just write it?
Yes. Written, designed and shipped, by the same four people. You are reading one of ours.
How many rounds of changes do I get?
Revisions come back the same day or the next, with no cap as long as there is a real reason. Three full rewrites are free; a fourth costs half the price of that piece.
What do you need from me to start?
What the piece is for, who it is talking to, and when you need it. If you have a brand voice or an SOP, send it. If you do not, we write one before the first post.
