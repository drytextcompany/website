Before you call

Questions people ask first.

Do you post it as well, or only write it?
We write. You, your team or your agency post it. If you want someone to run the page, schedule and reply to comments, we will say so rather than pretend we do that too.
How much does a month of social content cost?
It depends on how much you post. A post starts at Rs 1,200, a carousel at Rs 1,500 and a reel script at Rs 3,500, so a light month is a few thousand rupees and a heavy one runs higher. Send a month's brief and we will send a monthly estimate.
Is any of it written by AI?
No. Every caption, script and slide is written by us. For AI video concepts we write the idea and the script, and the tools only render the picture.
We are a new page with nothing on it. Where do you start?
With one page on how the brand should sound: who it talks to, its tone, and what it talks about. It takes a day and it is why post 1 and post 50 sound like the same company.
Do you write in Gujarati?
Yes, properly, not as a translation of the English. Bag lines, print, and a fortnightly newspaper concept, all written in Gujarati.
How fast can you turn a post around?
Normally in the next batch. Anything needed in under 48 hours is a rush job and costs 30 per cent more, which we will tell you before we take it.
