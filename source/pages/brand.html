The wordmark

Two versions of the same name.

the edited one has to earn its space.

Edited · the landing page, deck covers, our own posts, printed cards. Never on red, and never under 120px wide: the handwriting turns to mush.

Edited, stacked · for tall spaces: a poster, a phone screen, the back of a card.

Dry · everywhere else. Proposals, invoices, contracts, email signatures, co-branding, stamps, embroidery, one-colour printing.

Type

Three faces, one job each.

  • Newsreader for everything you read. Print-shaped, made for long text.
  • Kalam for the handwriting: our notes in the margin, and nothing else.
  • JetBrains Mono for labels, dates, prices and small print.
  • Noto Serif Gujarati when the words are Gujarati, which they often are.
