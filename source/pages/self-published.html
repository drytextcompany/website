NommNomm

Ordering and billing for cafes. The menu guests order from, the screen the counter runs on and the owner’s back office, all designed for one cafe and nobody else.

every qr menu in this city is a pdf that won’t zoom.

  • RituChai house. The menu follows the sky.
  • DialtoneRoastery. Turn a dial to suit your mood.
  • The Dog-EarBook cafe. The menu is a card catalogue.

Three invented cafes, the same software underneath

same software. you would never know.

Why not the POS you already have

Most POS software gives every cafe the same screens with a different logo. NommNomm starts from the cafe.

Made for one cafe

Every screen is designed for the room it runs in. Ritu’s counter is a floor plan where tables light up as orders arrive. The Dog-Ear’s stamps each order like a library book. Nobody else gets yours.

Written to sell

We are a writing studio first. The dish names and descriptions are written to get ordered, not copied off the kitchen’s list.

Regulars built in

Your usual, stamp cards, a customer book and win-back messages on WhatsApp come in the same system. No loyalty app bolted on the side.

the logo was never the problem.
